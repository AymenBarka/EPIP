# Release v1.2.0

## Highlights and modules

Added EPIP-012 Decision Engine as the sole authority for trading intent. It introduced
`TradeDecision`, `DecisionSnapshot`, rules, matrix/scoring, confidence, probability, execution
priority, decision quality, risk profile, rationale, entry/exit suggestions, events, graph, history,
and deterministic serialization.

## Architecture changes

Decision consumes aligned Market Context and Elliott snapshots. Downstream modules no longer infer
their own action from raw analysis; they consume the official immutable decision.

## Quality and tests

Black, Ruff, MyPy, and Pytest passed with 96% coverage. Tests covered actions, rule/scoring logic,
risk reasoning, events, engine integration, graph/history, and serialization.

## Benchmarks

A Decision benchmark was added. Results remain a local engineering baseline rather than a
cross-platform service-level guarantee.
