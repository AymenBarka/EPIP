## Architecture and behavior

A07 public outputs are immutable, runtime-hashable, deterministic, reconstructable, and
replay-safe. Malformed inputs, continuity contradictions, invalid predecessor state, and tampered
reconstruction fail closed. Final E09 signals copy canonical identity, policy reference, direction,
entry, stop, target, risk, reward, RR, confidence, evaluation timestamp, and expiration without
recomputation.

The final signal boundary is broker-agnostic and execution-independent. A07 does not perform order
execution, broker or MT5 integration, position management, lot sizing, slippage handling, or live
wall-clock expiry checks.
