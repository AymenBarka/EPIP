# Decision Vocabulary

## Terms

- **Evidence:** attributable input with explicit provenance and quality measures.
- **Hypothesis:** testable interpretation supported or contradicted by evidence.
- **Scenario:** coherent possible state composed from hypotheses.
- **Candidate:** possible decision action subject to constraints.
- **Constraint evaluation:** immutable acceptance result owned by an external policy domain.
- **Recommendation:** proposed disposition of one candidate.
- **Decision:** final immutable outcome with context and explanation.
- **Snapshot:** versioned capture of a decision.
- **Confidence:** degree of belief in an assessment.
- **Quality:** fitness and reliability of the underlying information.
- **Validity:** explicit assessment of whether an object remains applicable.
- **Uncertainty:** residual unknown information, expressed from zero to one.

## Example

Market data can be represented as evidence, referenced by a directional hypothesis, grouped into a bullish scenario, and attached to a long candidate. A risk constraint may accept that candidate before a recommendation and decision are recorded. Programme A defines these records only; it does not perform these evaluations.
